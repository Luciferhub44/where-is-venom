# Where Is Venom?

*A true story of faith & recovery.*

**[whereisvenom.com](https://whereisvenom.com)** · Instagram [@queen_xtelle](https://www.instagram.com/queen_xtelle) · TikTok [@queen.xtelle](https://www.tiktok.com/@queen.xtelle)

For over two years, people kept asking: *"Where is Venom?"* Some thought he'd left the country. Some thought he'd stopped creating. Almost no one knew the truth.

Two years ago, Venom's life changed in a matter of seconds. What followed was a season of comas, uncertainty, endless surgeries, and prayer — the story his sister, Queen Xtelle, was never ready to tell. Until now.

## The Story

Twelve episodes. One miracle. From the silence and the rumors to the darkest day and the long road back — the full journey, told honestly, in Venom's family's own words.

1. **The Silence** — the questions everyone was asking, and the story no one had heard.
2. **The Day Everything Changed** — the accident, told honestly, two years later.
3. **Every Second Mattered** — emergency rooms, beeping machines, and a fight for hope.
4. **The Darkest Day** — *"Your brother is in a coma."*
5. **Hope Was All We Had** — the longest days of waiting, and the only language that made sense.
6. **Harder to Edit Than the Last** — why every episode gets heavier to relive.
7. **Healing Isn't Always Loud** — what recovery actually looks like.
8. **Episode 8** — the moments that become more precious in hindsight.
9. **Some Days, Hope Was Enough**
10. **"Just One More Surgery"** — the surgery that was never really just one.
11. **Some Memories Are Still Hard to Revisit** — some of these are still hard to sit down and edit.
12. **Twelve Episodes Later — Thank You** — every prayer, every share, every act of kindness. But the story isn't over — Venom is still fighting to get to his next surgery.

Watch every episode at **[whereisvenom.com](https://whereisvenom.com/#story)**.
