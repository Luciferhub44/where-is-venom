### His Story. His Life. His Legacy.

A brother. A son. A father. A life that touched so many people.

This website originally documented Venom's medical journey when our family was fighting and praying for his recovery. Venom has since passed away. Today, this space stands as a permanent digital home to honour his life, celebrate who he was beyond the hospital walls, and preserve the memories our family and community will hold close forever.

---

## Website Structure

1. **A Life Remembered (Hero)**: Introduces Venom with authentic photography, celebrating his life as a creator, father, son, and brother.
2. **The Journey Archive (12 Episodes)**: Preserves the original 12 video episodes documenting the family's journey, accompanied by a respectful visitor notice.
3. **Beyond the Journey**: Celebrates his personality, creative craft (#venomzcustomz), family bonds, and photographs.
4. **His Story Doesn't End Here & Share a Memory**: A moderated space inviting friends and supporters to submit personal memories and tributes for family review.
5. **To Everyone Who Walked With Us**: A permanent expression of heartfelt gratitude to everyone who prayed, encouraged, and supported the family.
6. **Private Admin Portal (`/admin`)**: Password-gated dashboard for the family to review incoming memorial submissions and view historical campaign records.
